### Interim Short Description:
Early closed-test build of Parity. Account sign-in works; more features soon.

### Interim Full Description:
Parity is an app for keeping a shared expense and payment ledger between two people. This is an early closed-test build, and it is not feature-complete yet.

What works in this build
Right now you can create an account, sign in, view your profile, and sign out. That is the whole app at this stage. The point of this test is to shake out account creation, sign-in, staying signed in between sessions, and general stability before the main features are added.

What is not here yet
The core of Parity, recording expenses and payments, confirming them with another person, and tracking a running balance, is not in this build. Those screens are still being built and will arrive in later test releases. If you sign in and land on a nearly empty home screen, that is expected, not a bug.

What would help to test
Please try creating an account, signing in and out, and closing and reopening the app to confirm you stay signed in. If anything fails, hangs, crashes, or behaves oddly, that is exactly what is useful to hear about.
