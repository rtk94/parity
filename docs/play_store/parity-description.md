### Short Description:
Shared expenses and payments between two people, confirmed by both.

### Full Description:
Parity is a straightforward ledger for two people who share expenses. Split a dinner, cover a bill, pay each other back, and keep a clear running balance of who owes whom. No group trips, no ten-way splits, no clutter. Just an honest record between two people.

Both people confirm every entry
Nothing affects your balance until the other person agrees to it. When you record an expense or a payment, it stays pending until your counterpart confirms it. If it does not match what they remember, they can discard it. Your balance only ever reflects entries you both agreed on, so there is no silent drift and no one-sided bookkeeping.

A ledger that does not rewrite itself
Parity's records are append-only. Once an entry is confirmed, it is not edited or deleted behind the scenes. If something was wrong, you reverse it: Parity creates a matching reversing entry, which the other person also confirms, and then you record the correct version. The original stays visible. The history is always a true account of what happened and what was corrected, never a version that quietly changed after the fact.

One currency per shared ledger
Each connection between you and another person has its own currency, chosen when you set it up. Everything in that ledger is recorded in that currency, with no conversion. Every amount is stored exactly, to the cent.

Hosted, nothing to run yourself
This version of Parity is a hosted service. You create an account, connect with another person, and start recording. There is no server to set up or maintain.

What Parity is not
Parity does not move money. It is not a payment app, a bank, or an escrow service, and it does not process payments or settle up for you. You pay each other however you already do; Parity just keeps the record straight. It does not arbitrate disputes either: it is a shared notebook that two people keep honest, not a referee.

The idea
Most expense-splitting apps try to do everything for everyone. Parity does one thing: keep a clear, agreed, tamper-evident record of expenses and payments between two people. If you need group trips, receipt scanning, or budgeting categories, this is not that app. If you just need two people to agree on who owes whom, it should feel simple.
