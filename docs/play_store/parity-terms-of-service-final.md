## 2. What Parity is

Parity is a record-keeping tool for tracking shared expenses and payments
between two connected users. It lets you record what each of you spent or
paid, confirm those records with each other, and see the resulting balance
between you.

**Parity is only a record of what you and the other user tell it.** To be
clear about what Parity is not:

- It is **not** a payment processor. It does not move, hold, transfer, or
  process money.
- It is **not** a bank, escrow service, or custodian of funds.
- It is **not** a dispute-resolution service or arbiter. We do not decide
  who is right when two users disagree about what they owe each other.

Any actual settling-up (sending money to the other person) happens entirely
outside Parity, through whatever means you and the other user choose.
