# Delete your Parity account

You can ask us to delete your Parity account and the personal data
associated with it at any time.

## How to request deletion

Send an email to richardtknepp@gmail.com from an address you can be
contacted at, including the username of the account you want deleted. We
will confirm the request before acting on it, so that no one else can
delete your account.

## What gets deleted

Your username, display name, and password are permanently removed, and
your account can no longer be signed into.

Expense and payment records you shared with another person are kept in
that person's ledger, with your identifying information removed, because
those records are also their financial history with you. Your identity on
those records is replaced with an anonymized placeholder; the amounts,
descriptions, and dates themselves are retained so the other person's
ledger stays accurate.

## How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of confirming your
request.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
